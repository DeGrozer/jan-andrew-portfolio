# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js with React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, GSAP, and Three.js.

## Users

Recruiters and potential freelance clients evaluating Jan Andrew Barte's web development and frontend work.

## Product Purpose

This portfolio presents Jan Andrew Barte's projects, technical capabilities, background, and contact paths. Success means visitors understand what he builds, trust the quality of the work, and open a project or contact him.

## Positioning

The portfolio combines practical web systems and frontend craft with interactive visual storytelling, data-oriented experiments, and personal research interests.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive on the landing page to evaluate capability and fit. They may browse selected work, inspect project details and technologies, learn about Jan's background, explore personal interests, and contact him or view his CV.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The landing page includes selected projects, technology information, about and education content, explorations, contact links, and CV access.
- Project links, external profiles, resume access, light/dark themes, and interactive/simple experience modes must remain functional.
- The redesign must preserve existing project content, factual claims, links, and accessibility.
- The implementation remains a responsive web experience with reduced-motion support.

## Brand Commitments

- The existing name is Andrew Barte / Jan Andrew Barte.
- The voice is personal, thoughtful, direct, and lightly playful.
- Existing project imagery, portrait, resume, and external profile links are supplied assets and must not be replaced with invented proof.

## Evidence on Hand

- Selected project data and links: `lib/data/projects.ts`
- Site profile and navigation: `lib/data/site.ts`
- Portrait: `public/images/portrait/barte.webp`
- Project imagery: `public/images/projects/`
- Resume: `public/Barte - Resume/Jan Andrew Barte - Resumé.pdf`
- About and education content: `app/page.tsx`

## Product Principles

- Show real work before decorative experimentation.
- Make capability legible within seconds.
- Let project evidence carry trust.
- Keep personality without obscuring navigation or calls to action.
- Preserve accessible, responsive paths through the experience.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The landing page must support keyboard navigation, visible focus, responsive layouts, reduced motion, readable contrast, and touch-friendly controls.
